## About

[The AIMS Hackathon 2025](https://fundacionpasoslibres.org/aimshackathon/) was a global online
innovation competition organised by [Mila — Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/),
[Queensland University of Technology](https://www.qut.edu.au/), and
[Fundación Pasos Libres](https://fundacionpasoslibres.org/). It brought together developers,
entrepreneurs, researchers, and human rights advocates to build AI-driven solutions against modern
slavery.

Figures from the [Final Report](https://fundacionpasoslibres.org/wp-content/uploads/AIMS-Hackathon-Final-Report-2025.pdf):

| | |
|---|---|
| Participants | **227** from **38 countries** |
| Teams | **51**, of four to five participants each |
| Final solutions submitted | **23** |
| Judging | each solution scored independently by **at least six judges** |
| Would recommend the event | **95.9%** of surveyed participants |
| Would participate again | **87.7%** |

**Goals**

- 🌍 Raise public awareness of modern slavery
- 🎓 Build participants' technical and domain expertise
- 🤝 Foster interdisciplinary collaboration
- 🚀 Accelerate adoption of open-source Project AIMS tools

## 🌟 Impact

The hackathon brought multidisciplinary teams from around the world together to raise awareness of
modern slavery and human trafficking, develop AI-driven approaches to compliance analysis, build
bridges between technologists and human rights advocates, and advance open-source tooling for
corporate transparency and accountability.

Full detail is in the **[AIMS Hackathon Final Report 2025](https://fundacionpasoslibres.org/wp-content/uploads/AIMS-Hackathon-Final-Report-2025.pdf)** (PDF).
